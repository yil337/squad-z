Frequently asked questions

常见问题

How do I start playing? 怎么开始玩？

Tap START on the title screen. You'll arrive at your squad's home camp — follow the opening scenes, and your first trip out is a short tutorial run. After that, walk to the squad vehicle parked outside the gate and tap HEAD OUT? to pick a mode, a save slot and your loadout, then hit the road.

在开屏页点开始，你会来到小队的家园营地——跟着开场剧情走，第一次出门是一段简短的教程。之后走到大门外停着的小队车旁，点出发？，选择模式、存档和装备就能上路。

What are Dog Tags, and do I have to pay for them? 狗牌是什么？必须花钱买吗？

Dog Tags are Z Squad's permanent currency. You spend them on things like weapon attachments, recruiting and upgrading companions, and unlocking skills. You earn them just by playing — from bosses, achievements, daily missions and more — and the Campsite gives new players a free Welcome Gift.

The Campsite (from the main menu or the button at the top of your home screen) also sells Dog Tag packs as optional in-app purchases, including a one-time Starter Pack. Buying only gets you there faster; everything can be earned without paying. Prices are shown in your local currency before you confirm.

狗牌是 Z Squad 的永久货币，可以用来买武器配件、招募和升级同伴、解锁技能等。正常游玩就能获得——打 Boss、完成成就、每日任务等都会给；新玩家在营地还能免费领一份新手礼包。

营地（主菜单或家园顶部按钮进入）也出售狗牌礼包作为可选内购，其中包括一次性的首充礼包。购买只是让你更快拿到，所有东西不花钱也都能靠玩获得。确认购买前会以你所在地区的货币显示价格。

I bought Dog Tags but didn't receive them. 我买了狗牌但没到账。

"Waiting for approval…" — the purchase needs approval through Apple's Ask to Buy (Family Sharing). Once a parent or guardian approves it, the Dog Tags are added automatically the next time Z Squad is open. Nothing is charged until it's approved.

The connection dropped or the app closed mid-purchase — reopen Z Squad with an internet connection and give it a minute. The App Store re-delivers any unfinished purchase, and the game adds the Dog Tags automatically. Each purchase is only ever delivered once, so you won't be double-counted.

Still missing? Email us with the order ID from reportaproblem.apple.com (or your Apple receipt email) and we'll sort it out.

显示"等待批准…"——这笔购买需要通过 Apple 的"购买前询问"（家人共享）批准。家长或监护人批准后，下次打开 Z Squad 时狗牌会自动到账。批准前不会扣款。

购买途中断网或游戏被关掉——联网后重新打开 Z Squad，稍等一会儿。App Store 会重新送达未完成的购买，游戏会自动把狗牌加上。每笔购买只会到账一次，不会重复计算。

仍然没到？请把 reportaproblem.apple.com（或 Apple 收据邮件）里的订单号发邮件给我们，我们来处理。

Will my Dog Tags and progress carry over if I reinstall or switch devices? 删掉重装或换手机，狗牌和进度还在吗？

Your progress — saves, home, unlocks and Dog Tags — is stored on your device, not on an online account. It moves to a new iPhone or iPad when you transfer or restore from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes this data. The exception is Dog Tags you bought: if you're signed in to iCloud, the game remembers how many you've purchased and gives them back after a reinstall or on another device. Dog Tags earned by playing don't come back this way. If something went wrong, email us and we'll do our best to help.

你的进度——存档、家园、解锁内容和狗牌——保存在你的设备上，而不是某个在线账号里。用 iCloud 或电脑备份迁移 / 恢复到新的 iPhone 或 iPad 时会一起带过去。删除 App 会删除这些数据。例外是你花钱买的狗牌：只要登录了 iCloud，游戏会记住你一共买过多少，删掉重装或换设备后会补回来。玩游戏得到的狗牌不在此列。如果出了问题，请发邮件给我们，我们会尽力帮忙。

How do I play local 2-player co-op? 怎么玩本地双人联机？

You need two iPhones or iPads, each with Z Squad updated to the same version, and both players must have finished the tutorial. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both and keep the devices close — no internet and no shared Wi-Fi network are needed. At home, walk to the vehicle outside the gate, tap HEAD OUT? → DOUBLE PLAYER and choose your loadout. One player taps CREATE ROOM, the other JOIN ROOM. Allow "Local Network" access when iOS asks.

需要两台 iPhone 或 iPad，都更新到同一版本的 Z Squad，并且两人都已完成教程。两台都打开 Wi-Fi 和蓝牙并放在一起——不需要联网，也不需要连同一个 Wi-Fi。在家园走到大门外的车旁，点出发？→ 双人并选好装备，一人点开房间，另一人点加入房间。iOS 询问时请允许"本地网络"权限。

I denied "Local Network" access, or the two devices can't find each other. 我拒绝了"本地网络"权限 / 两台设备找不到对方。

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Z Squad (you can also find it under Settings → Z Squad). Do this on both devices, then fully close and reopen the game. Also check that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on (not just disconnected in Control Center), Airplane Mode is off, and both devices run the same game version.

This permission is used only to find and connect to the other player's device nearby. Z Squad doesn't use it to scan your network or send anything off your local connection.

打开 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 本地网络，打开 Z Squad（也可以在 设置 → Z Squad 里找到）。两台设备都要打开，然后彻底关掉游戏再重新打开。另外请确认 Wi-Fi 和蓝牙确实开着（不只是在控制中心里断开了连接）、飞行模式已关闭、两台的游戏版本一致。

这项权限只用来寻找并连接附近另一位玩家的设备。Z Squad 不会用它扫描你的网络，也不会把任何数据发到本地连接之外。

How do saves work? I lost my run. 存档是怎么回事？我这局没了。

Z Squad has four save slots. Your run is saved automatically — including when you switch away from the app — and picking the same slot when you HEAD OUT? again continues it. Each new day in a run is also kept as a checkpoint you can return to. When your squad falls, that run is over and its slot starts a new one; that's the roguelike part. Your home, Dog Tags and unlocks are permanent and are never lost when a run ends.

Saves live only on this device and aren't synced between devices.

Z Squad 有 4 个存档位。跑局会自动保存——包括你切出游戏的时候——再次出发？时选同一个存档位就能接着玩。一局里每过一天也会留一个检查点，可以回到那一天。小队全军覆没时，这一局就结束了，该存档位会重新开局——这是肉鸽玩法的一部分。家园、狗牌和解锁内容是永久的，跑局结束也不会丢。

存档只保存在本机，不会在设备之间同步。

How do I change the language? 怎么切换语言？

The game is available in English and Simplified Chinese. On first launch it follows your device language (Chinese → Simplified Chinese, anything else → English). You can switch anytime in Settings → LANGUAGE, from the main menu or the pause menu.

游戏支持英文和简体中文。第一次打开时会跟随设备语言（中文系统 → 简体中文，其他 → 英文）。之后可随时在主菜单或暂停菜单的设置 → 语言里切换。

Does the game work offline? 游戏能离线玩吗？

Yes. Single-player and local co-op both work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and achievements, buying Dog Tags, and sending a message from the Campsite. Scores earned offline are kept and uploaded the next time you're online.

可以。单人和本地双人都能完全离线游玩。只有 Game Center 排行榜与成就、购买狗牌、在营地留言这几项需要联网。离线时打出的成绩会先保存，下次联网时自动上传。

The leaderboard isn't loading. 排行榜加载不出来。

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center, which needs an internet connection and a signed-in Game Center account. Check iOS Settings → Game Center, and make sure Settings → UPLOAD SCORES is on in the game. If it still fails, sign out of Game Center and back in, then relaunch the app.

排行榜和成就基于 Apple Game Center，需要联网并登录 Game Center。请检查 iOS 设置 → Game Center，并确认游戏内设置 → 上传分数已开启。仍然不行的话，退出 Game Center 再重新登录，然后重启游戏。

How do I send feedback from inside the game? 怎么在游戏里给开发者留言？

Open the Campsite and choose LEAVE A MESSAGE. Messages are sent through Apple's iCloud, so you need to be signed in to iCloud and online. We read every one, but we can't reply inside the game — if you'd like an answer, email us instead. See the Privacy Policy for what a message includes.

打开营地，选择留个言。留言通过 Apple iCloud 发送，所以需要登录 iCloud 并联网。每条我们都会看，但没办法在游戏里回复——想要回信的话请直接发邮件。留言包含哪些信息见下方隐私政策。

I want a refund. 我想退款。

All App Store purchases, including Dog Tag packs, are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than the developer. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and submit a request.

所有 App Store 购买（包括狗牌礼包）都由 Apple 处理，因此退款也由 Apple 受理，而不是开发者。请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com，用你的 Apple 账户登录后提交申请。

I found a bug, or the game crashed or froze. 我发现了 bug / 游戏闪退或卡住了。

Force-quit the app and reopen it; your run is saved automatically, so you'll pick up close to where you were. If it keeps happening, restart your device and make sure the game is up to date. Then email contact@zsquad.app with what you were doing when it happened (e.g. "during a boss fight in co-op"), your device model and iOS version. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot. We track every report and ship fixes in updates.

先强制退出游戏再重新打开；跑局会自动保存，你会从接近原来的地方继续。如果反复出现，请重启设备并确认游戏已更新到最新版本。然后发邮件到 contact@zsquad.app，告诉我们出问题时你在做什么（例如"双人打 Boss 时"）、设备型号和 iOS 版本。附上截图或录屏会很有帮助。每一份报告我们都会跟进，并在后续更新中修复。

Your saves, home, settings, unlocks, Dog Tag balance, scores waiting to be uploaded, and the transaction IDs of past purchases (kept so each purchase is delivered exactly once) are stored locally on your device. 设备上的游戏数据。你的存档、家园、设置、解锁内容、狗牌余额、待上传的成绩，以及过往购买的交易编号（用来保证每笔购买只到账一次），都保存在你的设备本地。我们不会收到这些数据。若你开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，它们会随设备备份一起保存；删除 App 时会一并删除。

Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game submits your scores (distance traveled and zombies killed, solo and co-op) to Game Center leaderboards and reports your achievement progress. Other players can see your Game Center nickname next to your scores, according to your Game Center settings. Game Center is run by Apple; we can see leaderboard entries (nickname and score) the same way players do. You can stop all uploads at any time with Settings → UPLOAD SCORES in the game, or by signing out of Game Center.

Game Center（可选）。如果你登录了 Apple Game Center，游戏会把你的成绩（行进距离与击杀数，含单人和双人）提交到 Game Center 排行榜，并上报成就进度。其他玩家可以看到你的 Game Center 昵称和成绩（具体取决于你的 Game Center 设置）。Game Center 由 Apple 运营；我们和其他玩家一样只能看到排行榜条目（昵称与成绩）。你可以随时在游戏内设置 → 上传分数关闭全部上传，或退出 Game Center。

The supporter thank-you wall (opt-in). If you decline, never answer, or aren't signed in to Game Center, nothing about you is published and your purchase works exactly the same. Your answer is stored on that device only.

支持者致谢墙（需你同意）。购买之后，如果你已登录 Game Center，游戏会询问你一次：是否愿意把你的 Game Center 昵称列在游戏内 CREDITS（致谢）页的"支持者"一栏。只有你点了同意，才会公开任何内容。同意后，游戏会把一个累计支持值提交到一个游戏内不展示的 Game Center 排行榜；致谢页只从中读取名字，也只显示名字——不显示金额、排名或数值。如果你拒绝、从未回答，或没有登录 Game Center，你的任何信息都不会被公开，购买效果完全一样。你的选择只保存在那台设备上。

In-app purchases. Dog Tag packs are sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, email, Apple Account or payment details. Your device receives a signed record of the purchase (which pack, a transaction ID and the date), which the game uses on-device to deliver your Dog Tags. Apple gives us aggregated sales reports that don't identify you. The game also keeps a small support total ("hearts") for the thank-you wall, and a count of the Dog Tags you've bought so they can be given back after a reinstall; both are synced across your own devices through iCloud key-value storage in your personal iCloud account, which we cannot read.

App 内购买。狗牌礼包通过 Apple App Store（StoreKit）出售。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到你的姓名、邮箱、Apple 账户或支付信息。你的设备会收到一份经签名的购买记录（哪个礼包、交易编号和日期），游戏在本机用它发放狗牌。Apple 只向我们提供无法识别个人的汇总销售报表。游戏还会为致谢墙记录一个小小的支持值（"爱心"），并记下你买过多少狗牌（重装后补回用）；这两项都通过你个人 iCloud 账户里的 iCloud 键值存储在你自己的设备之间同步，我们无法读取。

Messages you send from the Campsite. If you choose Campsite → LEAVE A MESSAGE, your message is saved to our app's Apple CloudKit database, where only the developer reads it. Each message includes: the text you wrote; your Game Center nickname (or "anon" if you're not signed in); the app version; and your device's language and region setting (for example "en_US"). CloudKit also attaches an anonymous identifier that Apple generates for our app — it doesn't reveal your Apple Account or email. Messages are never shown to other players. Please don't include personal details you don't want us to have.

你在营地发送的留言。如果你使用营地 → 留个言，留言会保存到本游戏的 Apple CloudKit 数据库中，只有开发者会阅读。每条留言包含：你写的文字；你的 Game Center 昵称（未登录则为 "anon"）；App 版本号；以及设备的语言与地区设置（例如 "zh_CN"）。CloudKit 还会附带一个 Apple 为本游戏生成的匿名标识——它不会暴露你的 Apple 账户或邮箱。留言永远不会展示给其他玩家。请不要在留言中写入你不希望我们知道的个人信息。

Local 2-player co-op. Co-op connects two nearby devices directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity. The "Local Network" permission is used only to find and connect to the other player's device. The two devices exchange gameplay data only (such as positions, actions, squad and loadout). Your device appears to the other one as a random name like "Z Squad 1234", never your device name. Nothing from a co-op session is sent to us or to any server.

本地双人联机。联机通过 Apple 的 MultipeerConnectivity，用 Wi-Fi 和蓝牙直接连接附近的两台设备。"本地网络"权限只用于寻找并连接另一位玩家的设备。两台设备之间只交换游戏数据（如位置、动作、小队与装备）。你的设备在对方那里显示为类似 "Z Squad 1234" 的随机名称，绝不使用你的设备名。联机过程中的任何内容都不会发送给我们或任何服务器。

Crash reports. The game has no crash reporting or analytics of its own. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymized crash reports and usage statistics with us, which we use only to fix problems.

崩溃报告。游戏本身不带任何崩溃上报或数据分析。如果你选择了与 App 开发者共享分析数据（iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进），Apple 可能会向我们提供匿名的崩溃报告和使用统计，我们只用它来修复问题。

Email and this website. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write; mail to contact@zsquad.app is forwarded by our email provider (Cloudflare) to the inbox we use for support (Google Gmail). This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. It remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage only. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

邮件与本网站。如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你写的内容；发往 contact@zsquad.app 的邮件由我们的邮件服务商（Cloudflare）转发到我们处理支持事务的邮箱（Google Gmail）。本网站没有 Cookie、数据分析或广告，只会在你浏览器的本地存储里记住你选的语言。网站托管方（GitHub Pages）可能为提供和保护网站而记录 IP 地址等技术数据。

About

关于

Z Squad is a top-down pixel-art zombie roguelite runner for iOS, made by an indie developer who loves the zombie genre. Free-to-play, no ads, with optional in-app purchases. Solo or local 2-player co-op on two devices.

Z Squad 是一款俯视角像素僵尸肉鸽跑酷 iOS 游戏，由一位热爱僵尸题材的独立开发者制作。免费畅玩，无广告，含可选内购。支持单人或本地双人沙发联机。

Built with Swift & SpriteKit. Currently in TestFlight, launching on the App Store on December 3, 2026.

用 Swift 和 SpriteKit 开发，目前 TestFlight 公测中，2026 年 12 月 3 日正式上架 App Store。
